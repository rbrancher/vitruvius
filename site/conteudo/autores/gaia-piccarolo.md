Architect, she just got her phd in “Storia dell’Architettura e dell’Urbanistica” at Politecnico di Torino with the thesis Architettura come impegno civile. Lucio Costa nel Brasile di Vargas (1930-1945). She is contributing to teaching activities at Politecnico di Milano, where she graduated in Architecture in 2005 with full scores. She also contributes to “Il Giornale dell’Architettura” and “Paesaggio urbano” and participated in the organization and planning of several exhibitions. She was co-editor in 2007 with Giuliana Ricci of the volume Luigi Manini (1848-1936), architetto e scenografo, pittore e fotografo (ISBN 97888-3660888-1) focused on the migration of ideas and models between Italy and Portugal at the turn of the twentieth century.
Her actual research interests concern Brazilian architecture of the first half of the twentieth century with special focus on Lucio Costa and on the relation of Brazil with the former colonizers in the identity-building process. On this subject the paper Lucio Costa's Luso-Brazilian Routes from the '20s to the '60s: Redefining the Equilibrium of "Center" and "Periphery" was presented in the Conference “Ambiguous Territories: Articulating New Geographies in Latin American Modern Architecture and Urbanism” held in Columbia University on March 27-28, 2009. She recently contributed to the conference “Brasília. Dal Plano Piloto a oggi” held in Università IUAV di Venezia on June 10, 2010 with a paper on Lucio Costa.
