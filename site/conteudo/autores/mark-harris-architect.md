Mark Harris is the founding principal of markharris Architects, Inc., an architectural practice located in Colorado Springs, Colorado since 1991 that specializes within the civic, institutional, and educational markets.  Before forming his private practice Mr. Harris apprenticed under Antoine Predock, FAIA, (APA), serving as project designer for several of the firms more noted early projects while also managing the Arizona office.

Mr. Harris’ work in private practice has received numerous national and international awards, including the prestigious 2003 American Architecture Award.  Utilizing a highly collaborative process that focuses on in-depth programming, his work has been noted as ‘visionary, bold, and evocative”, creating a “cohesive and probing architecture that is both powerful and highly symbolic”. The firm is known for provocative design and a high level of professional service; by 2007 over 85% of the firms work was from repeat clients.

Considered an “emerging voice in architecture”, he has been a visiting lecturer, conference speaker, and studio critic at major national and international universities and conferences. He is the recent recipient of the prestigious Ucross Fellowship.
