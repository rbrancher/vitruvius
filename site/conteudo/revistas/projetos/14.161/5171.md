<p class="text">Rafael and Diana Vi&ntilde;oly have redesigned the Park Avenue Armory's annual art and design fair. This coincides with the fair's broader geographical and historical curatorial concept as conceived by its new leadership, Artvest Partners, which has also re-named the event Spring Masters, New York.</p>
<p class="text">Rafael Vi&ntilde;oly's design for the exhibit booths centers on a hexagonal planning module that can be variably combined to encourage and accommodate an array of presentation strategies by the exhibitors. The booths are clustered in irregularly-shaped groups, yielding diagonal passages through the hall that are more varied and dynamic and ultimately more stimulating for the fair's visitors.</p>
<p class="text">Interior Designer Diana Vi&ntilde;oly uses a rich palette of deep, earth hues to create visual continuity throughout the exhibition. Tulip arrangements spread throughout the hall help link the fair to its seasonal and physical context and to the Park Avenue Tulip Festival, which runs concurrently next door to the Armory.</p>
<p class="text">The redesign represents the most significant change in the Armory fair format in three decades, and takes advantage of the dramatic setting and sweep of the Drill Hall to create a range of display options that respond to the diverse needs of participating dealers.</p>
